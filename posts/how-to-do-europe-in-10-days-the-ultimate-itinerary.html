How to Do Europe in 10 Days: The Ultimate Itinerary

StellarSurfer
26.06.2026 • Read time: 4 min

Dreaming of a European getaway but short on time? You are not alone. Many travelers assume that conquering the Old Continent requires an entire summer, but with meticulous planning, efficient transportation, and a well-curated route, you can experience the absolute best of Europe in just 10 days. From the romantic canals of Amsterdam to the artistic masterpieces of Paris and the historic ruins of Rome, this ultimate whistle-stop itinerary is designed for travelers who want maximum impact without burning out.

However, moving across multiple countries in a short span requires preparation. Before you lock in your flights, make sure you know what to pack for a European summer vacation so you can travel light, breeze through train stations, and avoid checked-baggage fees. Let’s dive into the ultimate 10-day European whirlwind tour.

Overview of the 10-Day Multi-Country Itinerary

To make the most of 10 days without spending your entire holiday inside an airport, focusing on a single high-speed train corridor is the smartest strategy. This itinerary connects three iconic cities: Amsterdam, Paris, and Rome, utilizing budget airlines and high-speed rail to maximize your sightseeing hours.

Day Destination Primary Highlight
Days 1–2 Amsterdam, Netherlands Canal cruises, Anne Frank House, Van Gogh Museum
Days 3–5 Paris, France The Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Montmartre
Days 6–10 Rome & Vatican City, Italy The Colosseum, Roman Forum, Vatican Museums

Days 1 & 2: Amsterdam – Channels, Culture, and Cycling

Your European adventure begins in the Dutch capital, a city defined by its 17th-century canals, narrow gabled houses, and relaxed atmosphere.

Day 1: Getting Your Bearings

Arrive, check into your accommodation, and immediately rent a bicycle or join a walking tour. Amsterdam is wonderfully compact. Stroll along the Herengracht and Prinsengracht canals, stopping for a traditional Dutch stroopwafel. In the afternoon, visit the Anne Frank House (book tickets months in advance!) for a deeply moving historical experience.

Day 2: Art and Architecture

Dedicate your second morning to the Museumplein. Choose between the Van Gogh Museum, home to the world's largest collection of the artist's works, or the Rijksmuseum, which showcases Dutch Golden Age masterpieces by Rembrandt and Vermeer. Wrap up your time in the Netherlands with a sunset canal cruise before grabbing an early dinner.

Days 3 to 5: Paris – Art, Romance, and Iconic Landmarks

Hop on an early morning high-speed Thalys train from Amsterdam Centraal, and in just over three hours, you will pull into Paris Gare du Nord. If you are traveling with a partner, you might find that the French capital rivals some of the top romantic destinations in Europe for couples, thanks to its candlelit bistros and illuminated bridges.

Day 3: The Heart of the City

Start your Parisian journey at the Île de la Cité to view the exterior of Notre-Dame Cathedral, then walk along the Seine toward the world-famous Louvre. Spend a few hours getting lost among antiquities and Renaissance paintings (don't miss the Mona Lisa). In the evening, head to Trocadéro for the quintessential view of the Eiffel Tower sparkling on the hour.

Day 4: Bohemian Montmartre and Le Marais

Wander the cobblestone streets of Montmartre, soaking in the artistic heritage of the Sacré-Cœur Basilica. Afterward, explore the trendy boutiques and historic architecture of Le Marais. Indulge in macarons at Ladurée or classic hot chocolate at Angelina.

Day 5: Versailles Day Trip

Take a quick 45-minute RER train ride out of the city center to the Palace of Versailles. Explore the breathtaking Hall of Mirrors and wander through the sprawling royal gardens before returning to Paris for a final steak-frites dinner.

Days 6 to 10: Rome – Ancient Ruins and Italian Feasts

Board a quick, budget-friendly flight from Paris to Rome (Fiumicino or Ciampino). Welcome to the Eternal City, where millennia of history sit side-by-side with vibrant modern life.

Day 6: The Imperial Era

Kick off your Roman holiday by walking the ancient paths of the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill. Conclude your afternoon inside the magnificent Colosseum, imagining the roar of the crowds from antiquity. Toss a coin into the Trevi Fountain at night to ensure your return to Rome.

Day 7: Vatican City and Prati

Cross the Tiber River into the world's smallest independent state. Tour the Vatican Museums, marvel at Michelangelo's masterpiece on the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel, and stand in awe beneath the dome of St. Peter's Basilica. Spend the evening eating bowls of authentic *cacio e pepe* in the lively Trastevere neighborhood.

Day 8: Baroque Rome and Gelato Crawl

Spend a leisurely day exploring Rome’s most stunning piazzas. Visit the Pantheon (the best-preserved ancient building in Rome), marvel at the Spanish Steps, and relax at Piazza Navona. Dedicate time to a proper Italian gelato crawl, sampling artisanal flavors like pistachio and dark chocolate.

Days 9 & 10: Hidden Gems and Departure

Use your final two days to uncover Rome's lesser-known wonders. Visit the Catacombs along the Appian Way, explore the Borghese Gallery and its stunning surrounding gardens, or simply relax at a neighborhood café with an espresso. On Day 10, pack your souvenirs and head to the airport, carrying memories of three countries, countless photographs, and a lifetime of stories.

Essential Tips for Surviving a 10-Day Whirlwind Trip

  • Book Tickets Early: Major attractions like the Colosseum, Anne Frank House, and the Louvre sell out weeks—sometimes months—in advance.
  • Travel Light: Carrying a single carry-on backpack or small roller suitcase makes navigating trains, cobblestones, and budget airlines infinitely easier.
  • Stay Centrally: While budget hotels outside the city center are tempting, spending hours on public transit eats into your precious 10-day window.
  • Download Offline Maps: Keep Google Maps or Maps.me downloaded offline in case your cellular data drops while navigating foreign streets.

Doing Europe in 10 days is an ambitious, fast-paced adventure that leaves you craving more—which is precisely why so many travelers find themselves returning year after year. Pack your bags, keep your schedule flexible, and get ready for the trip of a lifetime!
