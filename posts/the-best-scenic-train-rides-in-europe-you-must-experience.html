The Best Scenic Train Rides in Europe You Must Experience

AetherDrifter
05.07.2026 • Read time: 5 min

There is a distinct, unhurried romance to rail travel that modern aviation simply cannot replicate. As you watch rolling hills, snow-capped jagged peaks, and cerulean coastlines glide past your panoramic window, the journey transforms from a mere transit into the core of your vacation. Europe boasts some of the most spectacular railway engineering feats in the world, cutting through treacherous mountain passes, hugging dramatic sea cliffs, and chugging through deep, ancient valleys. Whether you are planning a grand summer grand tour or looking for inspiration on when is the best time of year to visit Europe, integrating a scenic train ride into your itinerary guarantees memories that will last a lifetime.

In this comprehensive guide, we explore the absolute best scenic train rides in Europe, detailing what makes each route extraordinary, practical tips for booking, and what you can expect along the tracks.

1. Glacier Express (Switzerland)

Often hailed as the slow express of the world, the Glacier Express connects two of Switzerland’s most glamorous alpine resort towns: Zermatt and St. Moritz. Spanning a breathtaking 291 kilometers, the journey takes roughly eight hours, allowing passengers to fully immerse themselves in the dramatic topography of the Swiss Alps.

The Route and Highlights

  • The Landwasser Viaduct: A stunning six-arched curved limestone bridge that plunges directly into a dark tunnel carved into the sheer rock face.
  • Oberalppass: The highest point of the journey, sitting at an elevation of 2,033 meters above sea level.
  • The Rhine Gorge: Often referred to as the "Grand Canyon of Switzerland," featuring steep white chalk cliffs and rushing turquoise waters.

The train features state-of-the-art panoramic carriages with windows that curve into the ceiling, ensuring you never miss a peak. If you are looking to combine this snowy wonderland experience with some milder climate escapes later, you might also want to research the best European destinations for winter sun to balance your seasonal travel plans.

2. The Bernina Express (Switzerland and Italy)

Another masterpiece of Swiss engineering, the Bernina Express crosses the Alps from Chur (or St. Moritz) in Switzerland down to Tirano in Italy. What makes this route particularly astonishing is that it achieves its dizzying heights without the aid of a single cogwheel.

Engineering Marvels Along the Way

The train climbs past roaring glaciers and alpine lakes before descending through dramatic loops and spirals. The most famous architectural feature is the Brusio Circular Viaduct, an open spiral bridge designed to reduce the gradient of the track, allowing the train to gracefully loop over itself.

3. Flåm Railway (Norway)

For sheer dramatic verticality, the Flåm Railway (Flåmsbana) in Norway is unrivaled. Running from the high mountain station of Myrdal down to Flåm nestled at the end of the Aurlandsfjord, this 20-kilometer stretch is one of the steepest standard-gauge railway lines in the world on adhesion tracks.

What to Expect

  • Twenty tunnels carved into the mountainside, eighteen of which were hand-dug.
  • A thrilling pause at the Kjosfossen waterfall, where passengers can step onto the viewing platform to feel the mist and witness a theatrical display.
  • Sweeping vistas of deep green valleys, cascading waterfalls, and traditional Norwegian wooden farms.

4. West Highland Line (Scotland)

Starting in Glasgow and stretching to Mallaig on Scotland's rugged west coast, the West Highland Line takes travelers deep into the melancholic beauty of the Scottish Highlands. This route is deeply intertwined with rich cultural heritage, often pairing well with itineraries that focus on the best historic sites to visit in Europe, such as ancient castles and battlefields scattered across the British Isles.

Iconic Sights

The absolute highlight of the trip is crossing the Glenfinnan Viaduct, famous worldwide for its role in the Harry Potter film series. As the train curves across the 21-pillar concrete viaduct, you are treated to sweeping views of Loch Shiel and the surrounding monument.

Summary of Europe's Top Scenic Trains

Train Name Country Key Highlight Duration
Glacier Express Switzerland Landwasser Viaduct & Zermatt views ~8 hours
Bernina Express Switzerland / Italy Brusio Circular Viaduct & glaciers ~4 hours
Flåm Railway Norway Kjosfossen waterfall & steep fjord descents ~1 hour
West Highland Line Scotland Glenfinnan Viaduct & rugged lochs ~5.5 hours

Tips for Booking and Enjoying European Scenic Trains

To ensure your railway journey is as smooth as possible, keep these essential tips in mind:

  • Book Well in Advance: Popular scenic trains like the Glacier Express and Bernina Express book out months ahead, especially during peak summer and winter holiday seasons.
  • Seat Reservations: Many scenic routes require a mandatory seat reservation in addition to your rail pass or ticket. Double-check the requirements before arriving at the platform.
  • Choose the Right Side: Research which side of the train offers the best views for specific segments of the journey so you can secure the ideal window seat.

Embarking on a scenic rail journey across Europe offers a profound connection to the landscapes you traverse. Pack your camera, secure your window seat, and prepare to view the continent from an entirely new perspective.
